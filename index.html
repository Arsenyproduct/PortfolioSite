About

I’m a product designer specializing in complex B2B and B2B2C products — where a solution can't be designed well without understanding the business process from the inside. I currently work at Yandex, designing partner tooling for the delivery service and operational products.

Before that, I was Lead Product Designer at Sberbank (Apr 2024 – Oct 2025), leading the B2B2C banking product SBOL.pro — from hypothesis to implementation, defending decisions in front of stakeholders based on interviews and A/B tests.

I also focus on interface accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) and building design systems from scratch — including one that grew to 180+ components.

RZD Analytics Workspace

Analytics · Enterprise

The initial brief called for a set of standalone reports — but that would only partially fix the real problem: fragmented data access across teams. I used an Impact/Effort framework to align stakeholders on a phased approach: the first modules shipped as production-ready parts of the eventual system, not throwaway prototypes — giving fast visible progress without re-architecting later.

SBOL.pro — Accessibility & Inclusive Interface

Sberbank · Accessibility

Designed an inclusive interface mode grounded in specific, testable criteria rather than intuition alone: an accessibility toggle that raises contrast, adds visible focus outlines instead of fill-only buttons, clarifies tab boundaries, and restructures input labeling — validated against WCAG 2.1 AA rather than general best-practice guesses.
